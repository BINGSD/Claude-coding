Formula sheet

Everything on one page. Formulas marked Learn are not in the formula booklet, so you must memorise them. Formulas marked Given are usually in the booklet, but boards differ, so check your own.

  • Log lawslog xy = log x + log y Learn

  • Trig identitiessin² + cos² = 1,  1 + tan² = sec² Learn
