5-minute warm-up
  1. Differentiate ln(3x).
    Answer

    1/x  (ln 3x = ln 3 + ln x)

  2. Find ∫ 2e2x dx.
    Answer

    e^(2x) + c

  3. What is cos(π/3) exactly?
    Answer

    1/2

  4. X ~ B(10, 0.4). Find E(X).
    Answer

    4

  5. A particle starts at 3 m s⁻¹ and accelerates at 2 m s⁻² for 4 s. Find its final speed.
    Answer

    11 m s⁻¹
