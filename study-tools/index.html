Quick test

Five short questions from across the course. If any take more than a minute, revisit that topic.

  1. Differentiate ln(3x).
    Answer

    1/x, since ln 3x = ln 3 + ln x

  2. Find ∫ 2e2x dx.
    Answer

    e2x + c

  3. Write down the exact value of cos(π/3).
    Answer

    1/2

  4. X ~ B(10, 0.4). Find E(X).
    Answer

    4

  5. A particle moving at 3 m s−1 accelerates at 2 m s−2 for 4 s. Find its final speed.
    Answer

    11 m s−1
