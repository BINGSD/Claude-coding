FAQ

Common questions

Which exam boards do you cover?

We teach AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR A, OCR MEI and WJEC/Eduqas. Your tutor will use practice questions written for your specific board.

Do you teach Further Maths?

Yes. We offer Further Pure (complex numbers, matrices, polar coordinates, hyperbolic functions) plus Further Statistics and Further Mechanics options.

Are lessons online or in person?

All lessons run live online with a shared digital whiteboard. Every session is recorded so you can rewatch it while revising.

When should I start?

The earlier the better, but it's never too late. Many students join in Year 12 to build foundations; others join in Year 13 for focused exam preparation.

What happens in the free trial lesson?

You'll meet your tutor, work through a short diagnostic on key topics, and receive a written summary of strengths and areas to improve. No obligation to continue.
